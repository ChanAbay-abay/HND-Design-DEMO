## Context

**What they do:**
HND Design + Build is a Cebu-based architectural and construction firm founded in 2021 by Principal Architect Henson M. Atillo, offering end-to-end design and build services for high-end residential, interior, and commercial projects across Cebu and Manila.

**Why this demo:**
Cold pitch. HND has zero web presence outside a Facebook page despite 5 years in business and a strong project portfolio (renders, real photos, testimonials). The angle is: a firm with this level of work and client trust is invisible to anyone searching online. This demo shows what that visibility looks like.

**Audience:**
Both high-end homeowners and property developers in Cebu and Manila considering a design + build partner.
